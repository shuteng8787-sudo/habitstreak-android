HabitStreak — Build Better Habits, Privately

HabitStreak is a no-frills daily habit tracker for people who want to build streaks without the noise of accounts, cloud syncing, or social features.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

✨ Fully offline. No account creation. No cloud sync. No internet required. Your habits and streaks live entirely on your device.

🔒 Zero tracking. We collect nothing. No analytics, no ads, no telemetry. We don't know who you are, and we like it that way.

📅 12-week heatmap. Visualize your consistency at a glance with a clean weekly heatmap showing your last 12 weeks.

🌍 6 languages. English, 简体中文, Español, 日本語, Deutsch, Français — switch language anytime in settings.

⚡ Instant. No loading screen. No login. Open, tap to mark today done, close. That's the whole experience.

WHY USE IT

If you've tried other habit trackers and felt bogged down by:
• Mandatory account sign-ups
• Cloud syncing you didn't ask for
• Notifications that nag rather than help
• Subscription paywalls for basic features

HabitStreak strips all of that out. It does one thing: track daily habits and show your streak. Forever free, forever private.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK

• Reading 30 minutes a day
• Drinking 8 glasses of water
• Sleeping 7+ hours
• Daily exercise or stretching
• Language practice
• Meditation
• Any custom habit you define

HOW IT WORKS

1. Add a habit (give it any name)
2. Tap once to mark today done
3. Watch your streak grow
4. See your 12-week consistency heatmap

That's the entire app. By design.

PRIVACY PROMISE

HabitStreak stores all your data using your device's local storage. We have no server. We have no analytics. We have no ads. We literally cannot see what habits you track, because the data never leaves your phone.

If you uninstall the app, your data is gone — there is nothing in the cloud to recover. We recommend taking periodic screenshots if you want a permanent record of your progress.

FROM GENESIS FORGE

Genesis Forge is an indie studio crafting tiny tools that respect your time, attention, and privacy. We make small apps for big impact.

Contact: genesisforge.contact@gmail.com
Privacy policy: https://shuteng8787-sudo.github.io/privacy/habitstreak.html
